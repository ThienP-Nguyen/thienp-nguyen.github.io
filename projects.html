Research portfolio

Projects

Selected research and course projects in wireless communications, signal processing, and applied machine learning.

Developing MATLAB Comprehensive Simulation for 5G/6G Wireless Communications

  • Contributed to a practical simulation guide for evaluating energy-efficient technologies in future wireless networks.
  • Developed technical content, examples, and simulation-oriented explanations.

Machine Learning-Based ML Detection for 1-Bit Large-Scale MIMO

  • Studied bias learning and dithering learning for low-resolution MIMO detection.
  • Focused on reliable detection with reduced CSI and implementation complexity.

Cooperative Multi-Hop Relaying in Wireless Communications

  • Developed analytical and MATLAB-based workflows for cooperative and multi-hop relaying.
  • Focused on outage analysis, diversity combining, and relay-assisted transmission.

Generalized Fading Channel Modeling and Performance Evaluation

  • Analyzed Rayleigh, Nakagami-m, Rician, cascaded Rayleigh, and alpha-mu fading channels.
  • Implemented Monte Carlo simulations for outage, BER, and capacity evaluation.

NOMA-Based User Selection in Random Wireless Networks

  • Studied SIC-based NOMA with user selection and power allocation.
  • Evaluated outage performance and fairness trade-offs through analysis and simulation.

Relay Selection in Cognitive Radio Networks

  • Investigated partial and full relay selection in underlay cognitive radio networks.
  • Derived and validated outage probability expressions under Rayleigh fading.

Physical Layer Security in Relay-Assisted Wireless Systems

  • Compared DF and RF relaying from a physical-layer security perspective.
  • Evaluated secrecy outage probability and average secrecy capacity.

Course Projects

  • Automated QR code recognition for Vietnamese citizen ID cards.
  • Automatic egg incubator using PIC16F877A.
  • Entropy analysis and Huffman coding in MATLAB.
