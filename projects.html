Research Projects

Developing MATLAB Comprehensive Simulation for 5G/6G Wireless Communications

  • Collaborate on the writing and development of a comprehensive guidebook on simulation tools for evaluating energy-efficient technologies in future wireless networks.
  • Contribute extensively to the content and structure of technical chapters, providing practical insights and illustrative examples for academic and industry use.
  • Provide a foundational reference for researchers and developers in green communications and 5G/6G simulation design.

Machine Learning-Based ML Detection for 1-Bit Large-Scale MIMO

  • Proposed bias learning and dithering learning for signal detection with 1-bit ADCs.
  • Enabled reliable detection without requiring detailed channel state information (CSI), thereby reducing system complexity, lowering energy consumption, and supporting cost-efficient massive MIMO implementation.

Global Talent Training Program for Copyright Management Technology in Game Contents

  • Contributed to the literature review process and identification of relevant research.
  • Handled implementation and troubleshooting of simulation programs.

Cooperative Multi-Hop Relaying in Wireless Communications

  • Developed a thesis-level research guide to help students and early researchers understand cooperative communications, diversity combining, and multi-hop relaying in wireless networks.
  • Built analytical and MATLAB-based performance evaluation workflows for relay-assisted transmission, with emphasis on outage analysis, cooperative forwarding mechanisms, and system-level modeling.

Generalized Fading Channel Modeling and Performance Evaluation

  • Designed as a thesis-scale study and learning guide for analyzing wireless channels under Rayleigh, Nakagami-m, Rician, cascaded Rayleigh, and alpha-mu fading models.
  • Derived outage-probability-based performance analysis and implemented MATLAB/Monte Carlo simulations, showing how channel statistics can be used to study reliability, BER, and average Shannon capacity.

NOMA-Based User Selection in Random Wireless Networks

  • Prepared a research-oriented guide on NOMA transmission in random wireless networks, where the base station selects the two strongest users and applies SIC-based decoding to improve spectral efficiency.
  • Developed outage probability analysis and simulation models to study the effects of SNR, user population, and power allocation, with additional focus on fairness trade-offs between near and far users.

Relay Selection in Cognitive Radio Networks

  • Developed a thesis-level guide for underlay cognitive radio systems with partial and full relay selection under Rayleigh fading and multiple primary-user constraints.
  • Derived and validated outage probability expressions, highlighting how relay selection improves spectrum utilization and coverage.

Physical Layer Security in Relay-Assisted Wireless Systems

  • Built a research guide on physical layer security using Decode-and-Forward (DF) and Randomize-and-Forward (RF) relaying over Rayleigh fading channels.
  • Compared secrecy outage probability and average secrecy capacity through analysis and simulation.

Entropy Analysis and Huffman Coding in MATLAB

  • Developed a MATLAB-based framework for discrete source modeling, symbol probability estimation, and entropy computation.
  • Conducted a comparative study of Huffman source coding by evaluating average code length, coding efficiency, and redundancy.
