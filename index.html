About

Research with a systems perspective.

I am a GKS scholar in Electrical and Information Engineering at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech). My academic interests center on analytical modeling, performance evaluation, and the design of emerging wireless communication systems.

My research interests span physical-layer security, backscatter and energy-harvesting communications, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, satellite communications, and machine-learning-enabled wireless systems.

Previously, I studied Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering at Ton Duc Thang University, Vietnam, and conducted research with the Wireless Communications Research Group.

Research themes

A focused set of topics around reliable, secure, and intelligent future wireless networks.

01 · Security

Physical-Layer Security

Secure transmission design and performance analysis against passive and active eavesdropping in fading wireless environments.

02 · Low power

Backscatter & Energy Harvesting

Low-power and battery-constrained communication architectures combining ambient energy harvesting and backscatter transmission.

03 · Smart propagation

RIS & Fluid Antenna Systems

Reconfigurable and adaptive radio environments using RIS, active/hybrid surfaces, and fluid antenna systems for diversity and coverage.

Satellite Communications

Analytical modeling of LEO/MEO and non-terrestrial links with emphasis on reliability, secrecy, fading, and integrated network design.

Research direction

Connecting theory with future 6G systems.
